How paper mode works
  1. Press Start on a module below. The timer runs in the top bar.
  2. Work on the pages with the pen: underline, cross out, solve.
  3. Bubble each answer on the answer sheet below.
  4. When time is up, press Check to grade the module. Explanations are at the end of the PDF.
