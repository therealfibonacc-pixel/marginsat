Four sample questions

Reading and Writing · Inferences · Hardest

Archaeologists excavating a Bronze Age village in Scotland found bronze tools but no traces of copper or tin ore, the two metals needed to make bronze, within 200 kilometers of the site. They did find molds for casting tools and drips of solidified bronze on the workshop floor. Because bronze can be melted down and recast without losing quality, the archaeologists concluded that the village's metalworkers most likely
Which choice most logically completes the text?
  1. Amined small deposits of copper and tin near the village that the chemical analysis failed to detect.
  2. Bimported all of their tools already cast and never worked bronze themselves.
  3. Cobtained finished bronze objects through trade and recast them into new tools.
  4. Dmade most of their tools from a local metal and used imported bronze only for decoration.
Show the answer
Answer: CThree facts must fit together: no ore nearby, casting evidence on site, and bronze can be recast without losing quality. Only recasting traded bronze satisfies all three. A contradicts the absence of ore, B ignores the molds and drips, and D contradicts the bronze tools that were found.

Reading and Writing · Transitions · Hardest

Early in her career, the photographer Imogen Hart was told that portraits of factory workers would never sell. She resolved to prove her critics wrong. although she had little money, she spent three years photographing mill workers in their homes and at their machines, then exhibited the portraits in a gallery known for showing paintings by famous artists.
Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?
  1. ANevertheless,
  2. BIn contrast,
  3. CEven so,
  4. DTo that end,
Show the answer
Answer: DThe sentence describes the steps Hart took to reach the goal stated just before ("prove her critics wrong"), so "To that end" fits. The contrast ("although she had little money") is already handled inside the sentence. Trap: three choices signal contrast, but the relationship to the previous sentence is purpose.

Math · Equivalent expressions · Hardest

The expression 2x2+kx−104x2−25​, where k is a constant, can be rewritten as a quotient of two linear expressions by canceling a common factor. Which of the following could be the value of k?
  1. A−9
  2. B1
  3. C−3
  4. D3
Show the answer
Answer: BThe numerator factors as (2x−5)(2x+5), so the denominator must contain one of these factors. With 2x+5: (2x+5)(x−2)=2x2+x−10, so k=1. (With 2x−5: (2x−5)(x+2)=2x2−x−10, so k=−1, which isn't a choice.) For −9, −3, and 3, the denominator has no rational zeros, so nothing cancels.

Math · Nonlinear equations in one variable and systems of equations in two variables · Extreme

In the equation x−3x​−x+3k​=x2−918​, k is a constant. What is the sum of all values of k for which the equation has no solution?
Show the answer
Answer: 12Multiply both sides by (x−3)(x+3): x(x+3)−k(x−3)=18, or x2+(3−k)x+3k−18=0. Substituting x=3 gives 9+9−3k+3k−18=0, so 3 is always a root of this quadratic, and it is always excluded from the original equation. Factoring, (x−3)(x−(k−6))=0, so the only possible solution is x=k−6. It fails exactly when it is an excluded value too: k−6=3 or k−6=−3, so k=9 or k=3. The sum is 12.
