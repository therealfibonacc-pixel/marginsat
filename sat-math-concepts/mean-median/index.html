Means, medians, and changes to data

Totals, combined groups and the score you need next.

36practice questions
3levels

The key idea

Work with totals: total = mean × count. Adding or removing values changes the total and the count. The median depends only on the middle position, so extreme values move the mean but often leave the median alone.

The trap

Averaging two means without weighting by group size.

Can Desmos do it?

A calculator adds lists, but these questions rarely give you the list. Totals are the tool.

Level 1 · Warm-up

The mean of 7 test scores is 63. A new score of 33 is added. What is the mean of the 8 scores?
Show the answer
Answer: 237/4The original total is 7×63=441. Adding 33 gives 474, and dividing by 8 gives 59.25.

Level 2 · Test level

Class A has 13 students with a mean test score of 62. Class B has a mean test score of 81. The mean score of all the students in both classes combined is 74.5. How many students are in class B?
Show the answer
Answer: 25Totals: 13(62)+81b=74.5(13+b). So 6.5b=162.5 and b=25.

Level 3 · Beyond the test

Jordan's mean score on 3 tests is 84. What score does Jordan need on the next test to raise the mean of all 4 tests to 85?
Show the answer
Answer: 88The current total is 3×84=252. A mean of 85 over 4 tests needs a total of 340. The next score must be 340−252=88.
