Percent change, chained and reversed

Chained and reversed percent changes, and the right base.

36practice questions
3levels

The key idea

A percent change multiplies: up p% means ×(1+100p​), down p% means ×(1−100p​). Chain changes by multiplying the factors, and undo a change by dividing by its factor.

The trap

Adding percents that apply to different amounts, or undoing a 20% discount by adding 20%.

Can Desmos do it?

A calculator handles the arithmetic. Choosing the right base for each percent is the skill being tested.

Level 1 · Warm-up

A price is increased by 27%, and then the new price is decreased by 35%. The final price is what percent less than the original price?
Show the answer
Answer: 17.45Multiply the factors: 1.27×0.65=0.8255. That is 17.45% less than the original, not 8%.

Level 2 · Test level

A jacket is discounted 15%, and then 4% sales tax is added to the discounted price. The final cost is $97.24. What was the original price of the jacket, in dollars?
Show the answer
Answer: 110The final cost is the original price times 0.85×1.04=0.884. So the original price is 0.88497.24​=110.

Level 3 · Beyond the test

The population of town A is 25% greater than the population of town B. The population of town B is what percent less than the population of town A?
Show the answer
Answer: 20If B has 100 people, A has 125. B is 25 less than A, and as a percent of A that is 12525​×100=20. It isn't 25%, because the base is now A.
