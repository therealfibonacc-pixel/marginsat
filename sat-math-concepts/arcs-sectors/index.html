Arcs, sectors, and radians

Arc length, sector area, radians and inscribed angles.

36practice questions
3levels

The key idea

An arc or sector is a fraction of the whole circle: 360°θ​ in degrees or 2πθ​ in radians. In radians, arc length is rθ and sector area is 21​r2θ. An inscribed angle is half the central angle on the same arc.

The trap

Using the inscribed angle as the arc's measure, or mixing degrees and radians.

Level 1 · Warm-up

A circle has a radius of 4. An arc of the circle has a central angle of 45°. The length of the arc is kπ. What is the value of k?
Show the answer
Answer: 1The arc is 36045​ of the circumference 2π(4)=8π: 36045​×8π=1π. So k=1.

Level 2 · Test level

A sector of a circle with radius 3 has a central angle of 6π​ radians. The area of the sector is kπ. What is the value of k?
Show the answer
Answer: 3/4In radians, sector area is 21​r2θ=21​(3)2⋅6π​=0.75π. So k=0.75.

Level 3 · Beyond the test

In a circle, an inscribed angle measuring 35° intercepts an arc of length 935​π. What is the radius of the circle?
Show the answer
Answer: 10The central angle on the same arc is twice the inscribed angle, 70°. The arc is 36070​⋅2πr=187​πr. Setting this equal to 935​π gives r=10.
