Work rates

Jobs done together, fill-and-drain, and late starts.

36practice questions
3levels

The key idea

Turn times into rates: a job done in t hours means t1​ of the job per hour. Rates working together add; a drain subtracts. Work done = rate × time.

The trap

Adding the times instead of the rates.

Can Desmos do it?

Desmos can solve a1​+b1​=t1​ for you, but it can't tell you that rates, not times, are what add.

Level 1 · Warm-up

Pump A can fill a tank in 5 hours, and pump B can fill the same tank in 3 hours. Working together, how many hours do the two pumps take to fill the tank?
Show the answer
Answer: 15/8Pump A fills 51​ of the tank per hour and pump B 31​. Together: 51​+31​=158​ per hour, so the job takes 815​ hours.

Level 2 · Test level

Working together, Ana and Ben can paint a room in 6 hours. Working alone, Ana can paint the room in 15 hours. How many hours would Ben take to paint the room alone?
Show the answer
Answer: 10Together they paint 61​ of the room per hour, and Ana paints 151​. Ben's rate is the difference: 61​−151​=101​, so Ben alone takes 10 hours.

Level 3 · Beyond the test

Pipe A alone can fill a tank in 5 hours. Pipe B alone can empty the full tank in 9 hours. The tank is 21​ full when both pipes are opened at the same time. How many hours will it take to fill the tank?
Show the answer
Answer: 45/8The net rate is 51​−91​=454​ of the tank per hour. The tank needs 21​ more, which takes 21​÷454​=5.625 hours.
