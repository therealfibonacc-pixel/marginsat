Volume and surface area

Volume formulas, percent changes and displacement.

36practice questions
3levels

The key idea

Prisms and cylinders: base area × height. Cones and pyramids: 31​ of that. Spheres: 34​πr3. When dimensions change by percents, multiply the factors, squaring any that apply to the radius.

The trap

Forgetting the 31​ for cones, or not squaring the radius factor.

Can Desmos do it?

The formulas are on the reference sheet; the calculator only multiplies.

Level 1 · Warm-up

A right circular cone has a radius of 8 and a height of 12. Its volume is kπ. What is the value of k?
Show the answer
Answer: 256V=31​πr2h=31​π(8)2(12)=256π, so k=256. Leaving out the 31​ gives the volume of a cylinder.

Level 2 · Test level

The radius of a right circular cylinder is increased by 20%, and its height is decreased by 40%. By what percent does the volume decrease?
Show the answer
Answer: 68/5V=πr2h, so the volume is multiplied by (1.2)2×0.6=0.864, a 13.6% decrease. The radius factor is squared.

Level 3 · Beyond the test

A cylindrical container with an inside radius of 3 centimeters holds some water. A solid metal sphere with a radius of 2 centimeters is dropped in and is completely covered by the water, and no water spills. By how many centimeters does the water level rise?
Show the answer
Answer: 32/27The water rises by the sphere's volume spread over the cylinder's base: π(3)234​π(2)3​=2732​=2732​ centimeters.
