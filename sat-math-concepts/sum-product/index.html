Sum and product of roots

Sums, products and missing roots without solving.

36practice questions
3levels

The key idea

For ax2+bx+c=0, the solutions add to −ab​ and multiply to ac​. These give quick routes to sums, products, a missing root, and expressions like p2+q2=(p+q)2−2pq.

The trap

Using ab​ for the sum; the sign is negative.

Can Desmos do it?

Desmos finds the roots of a known equation, but the shortcut is faster, and it is the only route when a coefficient is unknown.

Level 1 · Warm-up

What is the sum of the solutions to 3x2−16x−13=0?
Show the answer
Answer: 16/3Since c<0 and a>0, the equation has two real solutions. Their sum is −ab​=−3−16​=316​.

Level 2 · Test level

In the equation 4x2+kx−72=0, k is a constant. If one solution is −9, what is the other solution?
Show the answer
Answer: 2The product of the solutions is ac​=−18. With one solution −9, the other is −18÷(−9)=2. You don't need k.

Level 3 · Beyond the test

The solutions to x2−10x−9=0 are p and q. What is the value of p2+q2?
Show the answer
Answer: 118p+q=10 and pq=−9. Then p2+q2=(p+q)2−2pq=100−2(−9)=118.
