Rates and unit conversion

Conversion factors, compound units and square units.

36practice questions
3levels

The key idea

Multiply by conversion factors written as fractions equal to 1, arranged so the units you don't want cancel. For areas and volumes, square or cube the length conversion.

The trap

Converting square feet to square yards by dividing by 3 instead of 9.

Can Desmos do it?

Desmos will multiply anything you type. Setting up which factor goes on top is the part that earns the point.

Level 2 · Test level

Carpet costs $36 per square yard. How much would it cost, in dollars, to carpet a rectangular room that is 12 feet long and 9 feet wide? (1 yard = 3 feet)
Show the answer
Answer: 432The room is 312​=4 yards by 39​=3 yards, or 12 square yards. (Equivalently, 108 square feet divided by 9.) The cost is 12×36=432 dollars.

Level 3 · Beyond the test

A leaky faucet drips 1 drop every 4 seconds, and 20 drops make 1 milliliter. How many liters of water does the faucet drip in one week?
Show the answer
Answer: 7.56A week has 7×24×3,600=604,800 seconds, which is 4604,800​=151,200 drops, or 20151,200​=7560 milliliters. Divide by 1,000: 7.56 liters.
