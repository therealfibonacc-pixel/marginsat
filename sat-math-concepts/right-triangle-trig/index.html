Right-triangle trig and complements

SOH CAH TOA, complements and scale factors.

36practice questions
3levels

The key idea

In a right triangle, sin=hypotenuseopposite​, cos=hypotenuseadjacent​, tan=adjacentopposite​. The two acute angles are complementary, so sinA=cosB and tanB=tanA1​. A ratio gives side lengths up to a scale factor k.

The trap

Treating the ratio's numbers as the actual side lengths.

Can Desmos do it?

Desmos evaluates trig functions, but a sine-equals-cosine question asks you to recognize complementary angles, not to solve an equation numerically.

Level 1 · Warm-up

In right triangle ABC, the right angle is at C and tanA=512​. What is the value of sinB?
Show the answer
Answer: 5/13Draw it with sides for angle A: opposite 12, adjacent 5, hypotenuse 13 (any multiple works, since only ratios matter; the missing side comes from 122+52=132). For angle B the roles of the legs swap: the side opposite B is 5 and the side adjacent to B is 12. So sinB=135​.

Level 2 · Test level

For the acute angles (4x+27)° and (3x+7)°, sin(4x+27)°=cos(3x+7)°. What is the value of x?
Show the answer
Answer: 8The sine of an angle equals the cosine of its complement, so the angles add to 90°: (4x+27)+(3x+7)=90. Then 7x=56 and x=8.

Level 3 · Beyond the test

In right triangle ABC, angle C is the right angle and tanA=2120​. The perimeter of the triangle is 280. What is the area of the triangle?
Show the answer
Answer: 3360The legs are 20k and 21k, and the hypotenuse is (20k)2+(21k)2​=29k. The perimeter is 70k=280, so k=4. The area is 21​(80)(84)=3360.
