Similar figures and scale factors

Scale factors for lengths, areas and volumes.

36practice questions
3levels

The key idea

If two figures are similar with scale factor k, lengths scale by k, areas by k2, and volumes by k3. Find k from whichever measurement you are given, then convert.

The trap

Scaling area or volume by k itself.

Can Desmos do it?

Desmos can't see the figures. Pick the right power of k.

Level 1 · Warm-up

Triangle ABC is similar to triangle DEF, with AB corresponding to DE. If AB=4, DE=6, and the area of triangle ABC is 32, what is the area of triangle DEF?
Show the answer
Answer: 72The scale factor from ABC to DEF is 46​, so areas scale by (46​)2=49​. The area is 32×49​=72.

Level 2 · Test level

Two cylinders are similar. Their surface areas are 25π and 16π square units, and the volume of the first cylinder is 625π cubic units. The volume of the second cylinder is kπ cubic units. What is the value of k?
Show the answer
Answer: 320The ratio of surface areas is 2516​=2516​=(54​)2, so the length scale factor is 54​ and volumes scale by (54​)3=12564​. So k=625×12564​=320.

Level 3 · Beyond the test

A right circular cone has a height of 6. A plane parallel to the base cuts the cone 3 units above the base. The piece between the plane and the base is what fraction of the cone's volume?
Show the answer
Answer: 7/8The small cone above the cut has height 6−3=3, so its scale factor is 21​ and its volume is (21​)3=81​ of the original. The bottom piece is the rest: 1−81​=87​.
