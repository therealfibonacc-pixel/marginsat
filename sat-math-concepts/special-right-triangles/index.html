Special right triangles

45-45-90 and 30-60-90 side patterns.

36practice questions
3levels

The key idea

A 45-45-90 triangle has sides s, s, s2​. A 30-60-90 triangle has sides s, s3​, 2s, with the shortest side opposite 30°. Equilateral triangles split into two 30-60-90 triangles, and squares into two 45-45-90 triangles.

The trap

Putting 3​ on the side opposite 30°.

Can Desmos do it?

These answers have radicals; Desmos gives decimals. Knowing the side patterns gets you the exact form.

Level 1 · Warm-up

A square has a diagonal of length 122​. What is the area of the square?
Show the answer
Answer: 144A diagonal splits the square into two 45-45-90 triangles, so the side is 12 and the area is 122=144.

Level 2 · Test level

A right triangle has a 30° angle and a hypotenuse of length 14. What is the perimeter of the triangle?
  1. A14+143​
  2. B21+73​
  3. C7+213​
  4. D21+72​
Show the answer
Answer: BThe side opposite 30° is half the hypotenuse, 7, and the other leg is 73​. The perimeter is 14+7+73​=21+73​.

Level 3 · Beyond the test

In triangle ABC, angle B measures 30° and angle C measures 45°. The altitude from A to BC has length 16. What is the length of BC?
  1. A32+162​
  2. B16+163​
  3. C16+162​
  4. D163​
Show the answer
Answer: BThe altitude AD splits BC into two pieces. Triangle ADC is 45-45-90, so DC=16. Triangle ADB is 30-60-90 with the altitude opposite the 30° angle, so BD=163​. Then BC=16+163​.
