No solution or infinitely many

When a linear equation has no solution or infinitely many, with constants to find.

36practice questions
3levels

The key idea

A linear equation simplifies to ax+b=cx+d. It has no solution when the x-coefficients match but the constants don't (a=c, b=d), and infinitely many when both match. Expand and collect before comparing.

The trap

Setting the constants equal when the question asks for no solution, or comparing coefficients before distributing.

Can Desmos do it?

Graphing both sides shows parallel lines (no solution) or one line (infinitely many), but only after you know the constant. With an unknown constant, compare coefficients by hand.

Level 1 · Warm-up

In the equation 3(x−8)−1=kx−12, k is a constant. For what value of k does the equation have no solution?
Show the answer
Answer: 3Expand the left side: 3x−25. No solution means the x-terms match and the constants differ. So k=3; the constants −25 and −12 are different, so the equation has no solution.

Level 2 · Test level

In the equation 5(6x+4)=ax+b, a and b are constants. If the equation has infinitely many solutions, what is the value of a+b?
Show the answer
Answer: 50Expand: 30x+20=ax+b. Infinitely many solutions means both sides are the same expression, so a=30 and b=20, and a+b=50.

Level 3 · Beyond the test
