Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. All five practice tests, the module drills, the redo drills and every report are free right now.

How close is this to the real SAT?

The timing, tools and module structure match Bluebook, and every question is tagged with College Board’s own skills. The questions themselves are written from scratch. See how the tests are made.

What’s the question bank?

A separate set of questions harder than the tests, none of them repeated from the tests. Browse it by skill, or start a Tailored set: it reads every question you’ve missed, on tests, drills and in the bank, and builds the next set around them. Open the bank.

Can I take a test on paper?

Yes. Open paper mode to write on any test with a pen, an Apple Pencil or a mouse, time each module and check your answers, or download the PDF and print it.

Can I stop partway through?

Yes. Save and Exit keeps your answers and the time left, and the clock stays paused until you resume from the home page on the same device.

Where is my progress saved?

In this browser, on this device. There’s no account to make, and nothing you do here is sent anywhere.

How accurate is the score?

Treat it as a close estimate. Each section’s raw score is converted with a fixed table. College Board also weighs each question’s difficulty, which nobody outside College Board can reproduce.

Is Margin made by College Board?

No. It’s independent practice made by Lamai Hons, who scored 1560 on the SAT.
